How It Works
Nobody can predict exact COLA — it changes every year based on inflation. The app uses a long-term planning average based on historical data, not a prediction of any single year. It's the same approach financial planners use: you can't know what inflation will be in 2034, but a reasonable average over 20+ years gives you a useful picture of where things are headed. Each year when the actual COLA is announced, the app's rate tables get updated through an app update on the App Store or Google Play.
Your SSA statement projects your benefit assuming you keep earning at your current level until you claim. The app uses the number you enter as your Primary Insurance Amount — the benefit at your full retirement age — and adjusts it for early or delayed claiming and future COLA. If your earnings change between now and when you claim, your actual benefit may differ. The app explains this in its Learn tab under Social Security Benefits.

Features & Roadmap
It's on the roadmap for the full Culminari app. Military retirement pay needs your retirement system, years of service, and retired pay to model correctly — it's a bigger build. This version focuses on VA compensation and Social Security benefits as the foundation, and military retirement is the next layer.
SMC-K shipped in v1.1 — enter up to three SMC-K awards in the wizard and they stack with your base rating in every projection. Higher SMC levels (L through T, plus combinations) have their own rate structures outside the standard 10–100% schedule; those remain on the roadmap.
TRICARE Select and Prime for military retirees are planned for the full Culminari app alongside military retirement pay. Getting retiree healthcare costs in there next to Medicare would make the picture much more complete for 20+ year retirees.
