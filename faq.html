How It Works
Nobody can predict exact COLA — it changes every year based on inflation. The app uses a long-term planning average based on historical data, not a prediction of any single year. It's the same approach financial planners use: you can't know what inflation will be in 2034, but a reasonable average over 20+ years gives you a useful picture of where things are headed. Each year when the actual COLA is announced, the app's rate tables get updated through an App Store update.
Your SSA statement projects your benefit assuming you keep earning at your current level until you claim. The app uses the number you enter as your Primary Insurance Amount — the benefit at your full retirement age — and adjusts it for early or delayed claiming and future COLA. If your earnings change between now and when you claim, your actual benefit may differ. The app explains this in its Learn tab under Social Security Benefits.
