How it works

Three steps to your arc

No accounts. No data collection. Everything stays on your device.

📋

Tell us about you

A short wizard asks the basics — your rating, dependents, state, and claiming preferences. Three steps, under two minutes.

See your arc

What we believe

Built different, on purpose

🎯
Projections, not advice

Culminari applies published government formulas to your inputs. We show you the math — we don't tell you what to do with it.

🏠
Your data stays yours

No user accounts, no analytics, no Catalem servers. Your financial information is encrypted on your device. The full app adds iCloud backup — your cloud, not ours. We never see your data.

📐
Honest, not precise

We show ranges and estimates — not false certainty. The future is uncertain. Your planning tool should acknowledge that.
