Privacy Policy

Culminari for Veterans · Catalem LLC

The Short Version

Culminari for Veterans (the App) does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your data. Your information never leaves your device. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. The Culminari website (culminari.com) collects email addresses only if you voluntarily sign up for launch notifications — we send one email at launch and nothing else.

Information We Collect — The App

Culminari for Veterans (the App) does not collect any personal information whatsoever. Specifically:

  • The App does not require or offer user accounts, logins, or registration.
  • The App does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information.
  • The App does not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data.
  • The App does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any form of analytics.
  • The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Information We Collect — The Website

The Culminari website (culminari.com) offers an optional email waitlist for launch notifications. If you choose to sign up, your email address is collected and stored by Mailchimp, our email service provider. We use your email address solely to send you a one-time notification when the app is available on the App Store. We do not sell, share, or use your email address for any other purpose. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email you receive from us. For information about how Mailchimp handles your data, see Mailchimp's Privacy Policy.

The website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics beyond what GitHub Pages (our hosting provider) collects as part of standard web hosting. We do not have access to GitHub Pages server logs.

Information You Enter

The app asks you to enter personal details during the wizard — such as your birth year, VA disability rating, state of residence, and Social Security information. This information is used solely to calculate your retirement income projections. It is stored locally on your device in the app's private sandbox, protected by iOS built-in data protection. It is never transmitted to any server, API, or third party.

Data Storage and Security

All data you enter is stored on your device only, using iOS data protection. When your device has a passcode enabled, this data is encrypted at the hardware level by the device's Secure Enclave. Culminari for Veterans does not implement its own encryption layer — it relies entirely on iOS built-in protections.

Network Usage

Culminari for Veterans makes zero network calls. All reference data (VA compensation rates, Social Security parameters, Medicare premiums, state tax rates, and county property tax data) is bundled within the app binary. The app does not connect to the internet for any reason.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no social media integrations, and no crash reporting services within the App.

The website (culminari.com) uses Mailchimp for the optional email waitlist and GitHub Pages for hosting. No other third-party services are used.

Children's Privacy

Culminari for Veterans is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Data Deletion

Because all app data is stored on your device only, you have complete control over it at all times. To delete all app data: use the "Clear All Data" option in the app's Settings, or simply delete the app from your device. When the app is deleted, all associated data is permanently removed by iOS. There is no residual data on any server because no data was ever transmitted.

If you signed up for the email waitlist on culminari.com and wish to be removed, click the unsubscribe link in any email you received from us, or contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update this page and note the revised effective date below. Because the app does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes — please review this page periodically.
